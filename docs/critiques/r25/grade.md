# r25 audit — grade

**Verdict:** blocker

## Summary
The falloff is far worse than "still blowing out the left third": measured analytically and confirmed against my own cold plates, **62% of the frame area is inside it**, and the chroma half-point sits 265–317 px from the frame edge against the real game's 57–103 px. It is purely screen-space Chebyshev — `tND`'s linear depth is bound in GRADE_FRAG and read 431 lines earlier for the ink floor, but the falloff ignores it, so a 12 m road at the edge is drained exactly like a 200 m hillside; vc-072 proves the real effect is `max(edge, farDepth)` because its far village drains to paper at the *centre* of the frame. Ink does not survive: the lift's `mix(0.45, 1.0, …)` floor hands every stroke at least 45% of the paper lift, so our drained margin measures p1 138–142 and 0.00% ink density against vc-104's p1 47 and 0.3–2.1%. The code comment claiming "ink at luma 0.15 keeps 85% of its weight" is arithmetically wrong — it keeps 50%. The p99 gap is the same bug: `uDrawFallAmt 0.66` caps the lift, so the margin tops out at p50 172 and the whole frame maxes at 238, while vc-104 puts 11.47% of its pixels above 225 (ours: 1.49%) almost entirely in the paper margin. Finally, the 5.16% ink on `tank` is **not** coming from the linework — short dark runs are already at parity with the reference (23k px vs 22k) — it is coming from *inside* dark masses, whose mean |Laplacian| is 15.97 against the reference's 3.7–4.1.

## Do not touch (verified good)
Do not touch these. (1) The decision to make the margin **rectangular/Chebyshev** rather than radial (canvasRenderPipeline.js:1897-1903) is correct and matches every reference frame — vc-104's margin is a straight-sided sheet edge, not a lens vignette. Keep it. (2) The centre/edge saturation ratio is genuinely on target for the first time in the project's history: my cold plates measure tank 2.48, closeup 2.71, overview 2.78, aim 2.36 against real 1.26–2.68. The *existence* of the effect is right; only its footprint and its treatment of ink are wrong. (3) `uPaperWhite 0xfaf4e6` is a correct cream and its luma is 244.3 — exactly the reference's p99 of 241–245. Nothing needs re-authoring there; the margin simply never gets there. (4) The sky exemption `fall *= mix(1.0, 0.35, sky)` at :1909 is the right instinct. (5) The r24 black-point work holds: our centre-box p1 33–34 sits on vc-088's 33 and vc-104's 32. The p1 gap in the whole-frame number is an artefact of the drained margin having no darks at all, not of the shadow end being wrong.

## Findings

### [blocker | small | verified] The drawing falloff covers 62% of the frame — 3–5× the real game's margin

**Location:** `src/render/canvasRenderPipeline.js:1906`

**Evidence:** Analytically: fall > 0 when max(m.x, 0.86*m.y) > uDrawFallStart/uDrawFallScale = 0.64/1.12 = 0.5714, i.e. 411 px in from each side of a 1920 frame and 181 px in from top/bottom. Union = 1 - 0.5714*0.6644 = 62.0% of frame area. Measured on my own cold plates (tools/shoot.mjs --cold), the x at which column saturation first reaches 50% of the centre value: tank 317 px (16.5%), closeup 282 px (14.7%), overview 265 px (13.8%). Real game, same measurement: vc-072 57 px (3.0%), vc-104 103 px (5.4%), vc-088 183 px (9.5%). The analytic model matches the plate exactly — closeup column sat is 0.300 at x=400 and 0.182 at x=320, straddling the predicted 411 px onset. Visually (closeup, left crop): the mid-distance farmhouse, the road, the retaining wall and the rock pile are all a flat cold-grey fog; in vc-104's left crop the half-timbered house is at full terracotta chroma from x=95 px onward.

**Proposed fix:** Shrink the footprint to a sheet margin. Set uDrawFallScale 1.12 -> 1.00 and uDrawFallStart 0.64 -> 0.84 (canvasRenderPipeline.js:2646-2647): the drain then begins 154 px from each side edge and reaches half strength at 77 px. Fix uDrawFallAniso 0.86 -> ~1.18 (:2649) — as authored it makes the SIDE margin 411 px and the top/bottom 181 px, i.e. the sides are 2.3× thicker in pixels; A = (1 - p/960)/(1 - p/540) is the value for an equal pixel margin on all four sides, ≈1.18 at p=154. Also note uDrawFallScale 1.12 clamps d to 1.0 for everything within 103 px of the edge, so the outermost 103 px is a flat 66% plateau with no sheet edge at all; with scale 1.00 the ramp runs all the way to the edge.

**Acceptance test:** node tools/shoot.mjs --cold tank /tmp/t.png && node /private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-raynos-projects-game-demos-valkyrie-chronicles-demo-opus/9bfd6309-a467-4f65-a389-55c29ed290ad/scratchpad/fall/fine.mjs /tmp/t.png — the reported 'left reaches 50%-of-centre' must fall to 60–120 px (currently 317) and the right likewise (currently 277). Re-run tools/artstats.mjs on the same plate: centre/edge ratio must stay in 1.5–2.7 (currently 2.48). Then look at closeup: the left farmhouse and road must be painted.

### [blocker | small | verified] Ink is erased by the drain: the lift has a 0.45 floor, so strokes get at least 45% of the paper mix

**Location:** `src/render/canvasRenderPipeline.js:1921`

**Evidence:** `float lift = fall * mix(0.45, 1.0, smoothstep(0.04, 0.62, lumaOf(c)));` — at an ink luma of 0.15, smoothstep(0.04,0.62,0.15) = 0.094, so mix(0.45,1.0,0.094) = 0.502, not 0.15. The comment on :1916 ("so ink at luma 0.15 keeps 85% of its weight") is arithmetically wrong by its own code: a stroke receives 50% of the full lift, i.e. at fall=0.66 it is mixed 33% toward uPaperWhite (250,244,230), taking an L38 stroke to L108. Measured on my cold plates: in a left-edge box (x 0-170) our closeup reads p1 138 / p50 211 for a NEAR road-and-wall and p1 142 / p50 176 for a far house — no dark line-art anywhere. Same box in vc-104 (far buildings, x 0-170 y 220-420) reads p1 47 / p50 193: a bright paper ground carrying full-value graphite. Ink density by 8x6 cell (dark & |lap|>12): tank columns 0 and 7 are 0.00% in every row; vc-104's column 0 is 0.54/0.32/1.00/0.70/2.06/0.47% and column 7 is 0.69/1.62/1.62/0.40/1.79/7.18% — the same order as its own centre.

**Proposed fix:** Make the ink exemption hard instead of floored, the way the wash quantiser's `draw` exemption already does at :1331. Replace the lift with `float inkKeep = 1.0 - smoothstep(0.06, 0.30, lumaOf(c)); float lift = fall * (1.0 - inkKeep);` so a stroke below display luma 0.06 receives zero lift and does not move at all, while a wash above 0.30 receives the full lift. The chroma drain on :1912 can stay as is — an ink stroke has no chroma to lose. Fix the :1916 comment while you are in there.

**Acceptance test:** node tools/shoot.mjs --cold tank /tmp/t.png && node /private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-raynos-projects-game-demos-valkyrie-chronicles-demo-opus/9bfd6309-a467-4f65-a389-55c29ed290ad/scratchpad/fall/ink2.mjs /tmp/t.png — grid columns 0 and 7 must each carry >=0.3% ink in at least four of their six cells (currently 0.00% in all twelve). And .../fall/band.mjs /tmp/t.png "L-edge" 0.00 0.08 0.10 0.85 must report p1 <= 70 (currently 135 on closeup, 138 on the near-road box).

### [blocker | small | verified] The margin can never become paper: uDrawFallAmt 0.66 caps the lift, and that is the whole p99 gap

**Location:** `src/render/canvasRenderPipeline.js:2645`

**Evidence:** `lift` is bounded by `fall`, and `fall` is bounded by uDrawFallAmt = 0.66, so the most the margin can ever be is a 66% mix toward uPaperWhite. Measured: our closeup left band (x 0-8%) has p50 172 and max 238; the same band in vc-072 has p50 201 and max 252, and vc-104's deckle strip (x<55) reads p1 180 / p50 225. Whole-frame: my cold tank measures p99 227 / max 238, with only 1.49% of pixels above 225 and the margin cells contributing 0.0-8.8%. vc-104 measures p99 241 / max 255 with 11.47% above 225, and the 8x6 grid shows those concentrated in the margin — top row 54.7/41.1/26.6/23.4/21.8/17.1/13.3/22.5%, bottom row 39.7/15.9/11.7/16.1/15.2/24.4/38.5/35.0%. Nothing in our tonemap clamps the top: uPaperWhite 0xfaf4e6 has luma 244.3 and the white point at :1358-1359 reaches it exactly at c=1.0; RANGE_FRAG is pass-through at uRangeAmt 0. The picture's own ceiling of ~238 is fine. The missing 1% of bright pixels is the paper the falloff never delivers.

**Proposed fix:** With the hard ink exemption from the previous finding in place, raise uDrawFallAmt 0.66 -> 0.95 and keep uDrawFallPaper at 1.0 (:2645, :2648). The wash then reaches genuine paper at the sheet edge while strokes are untouched; the two jobs the single 0.66 was trying to do at once are now separated. Note also that the vignette at :1871-1875 multiplies BEFORE the falloff, so with a full lift it is overwritten in the margin — that is fine and no change is needed there.

**Acceptance test:** node tools/shoot.mjs --cold tank /tmp/t.png && node tools/artstats.mjs /tmp/t.png — p99 must reach 238-245 (currently 227-228) and range 200-212 (currently 188), with centre/edge ratio still 1.5-2.7. Then .../fall/top.mjs /tmp/t.png: the fraction of pixels >225 must reach 6-12% (currently 1.49%) and must be concentrated in grid columns 0 and 7 and rows 0 and 5, matching vc-104's pattern.

### [blocker | medium | verified] The falloff has no depth term, so a near wall at the frame edge is drained like a distant hillside

**Location:** `src/render/canvasRenderPipeline.js:1904`

**Evidence:** `dEdge = max(m.x, m.y * uDrawFallAniso)` is a pure screen-space Chebyshev margin; nothing in the block at :1896-1924 samples depth. It is available and already used: `float distG = texture2D(tND, uv).a * uFar;` at :1465, 431 lines earlier in the same shader, for the depth-keyed ink floor. The reference proves the effect is two-term. In vc-072 the drain reaches the CENTRE of the frame in the far distance with zero screen-margin: measured on 20 vertical strips, strips 38-63% (the receding street and the far village) read sat 0.121-0.148 with p50 210-218, while equally-central near strips 18-33% read sat 0.212-0.256 with p50 111-133. A screen-margin-only term cannot produce that. Conversely in vc-104 the near geometry survives at the very edge: at x 95-230 the tank's green plate reads sat 0.130 / p1 39 (fully painted with full-value contour ink) while at x 0-90 immediately beside it the far buildings read sat 0.070 / p1 149 (pencil ghost on paper). Our closeup does the opposite — a NEAR road and wall at x 0-170 read sat 0.086 / p1 138.

**Proposed fix:** Make the falloff `fall = max(edgeTerm, farTerm)` and gate the edge term on depth so near geometry survives it. Inside the block at :1896, after computing `fall` on :1906, add (reusing the `distG` pattern from :1465): `float dz = texture2D(tND, vUv).a * uFar; float near01 = (1.0 - sky) * (1.0 - smoothstep(8.0, 45.0, dz)); fall *= mix(1.0, 0.15, near01); float far01 = (1.0 - sky) * smoothstep(70.0, 220.0, dz); fall = max(fall, far01 * uDrawFallAmt);` — near geometry at the sheet edge keeps 85% of its paint, far geometry drains anywhere in frame. The sky must stay on the existing :1909 path (distG reads 0 for sky, which is why :1466 already guards with step(0.0001, distG)). This is the P5 formulation finish_plan.md:196-200 actually specified ("as a function of screen-edge proximity AND far depth") and which r24 built only half of. Do NOT reach for the haze uniforms to do the depth half — that is ruled out at finish_plan.md:298.

**Acceptance test:** node tools/shoot.mjs --cold closeup /tmp/c.png, then .../fall/box.mjs /tmp/c.png "L-near" 0 560 170 200 "L-far" 0 250 170 200 — the NEAR road/wall box must reach sat >=0.20 and p1 <=70 (currently 0.086 / 138) while the FAR house box stays sat <=0.11 and p50 >=190. Then look at overview: the near trees at the right edge must be green, and the far village at the top of frame must still drain. Re-run tools/shoot.mjs --verify closeup to confirm no new state leak.

### [major | medium | likely] The 5.16% ink is texture inside shadow masses, not linework — cutting outline width is the wrong lever

**Location:** `src/render/materials.js:2136`

**Evidence:** Measured on my cold plates against the references. Horizontal dark-run distribution: our short runs (<=3 px) account for 23,037 dark pixels on tank, vc-104 has 21,740 and vc-088 14,942 — the linework is already at parity. What differs is mass: dark pixels in runs >=31 px are 51,596 on tank against 248,444 on vc-104, a 5× deficit. The excess ink is inside the masses we do have: taking dark pixels whose entire 5x5 neighbourhood is also below L70, mean |Laplacian| is 15.97 on tank and 15.14 on closeup, against 4.13 on vc-104 and 3.72 on vc-088 — and 54.5% / 52.2% of those interior pixels exceed the artstats ink threshold (dark & |lap|>12) against 5.1% / 3.5% in the references. Ratio of dark-mass interior to dark edge pixels: ours 0.36-0.46, reference 0.89-1.50. Two candidate drivers, both r24 changes: `mapFlat: 0` (materials.js:2136, was 0.92) now passes 100% of every albedo map's tonal deviation, which inside an unlit mass is pure noise; and `hatchStrength: 0.18` (src/core/config.js:84) plus `uGrain 0.45` (materials.js:1907) still run a substrate field in the darks via `col *= 1.0 + (pap - 0.60) * uGrain * pm` (materials.js:1609).

**Proposed fix:** Do not touch uOutlineWidth (1.35) or the crease weight — the reference has as many thin strokes as we do, and the rubric scores a hairline uniform edge <=3. Instead gate the albedo-map deviation and the substrate grain on scene luminance so they fade out below the shadow terminator, the mirror of what vcPaperMidScene already does at the highlight end (shaderLib.js:157). Concretely: multiply the mapFlat contribution and the uGrain term by smoothstep(0.06, 0.22, vcLum(col)) so a dark mass is smooth paint and a lit surface keeps its texture. Ablate first to attribute: render `tank` cold at mapFlat 0.92, then at CFG.render.hatchStrength 0, then at uGrain 0, measuring dark-mass interior |lap| each time — I could not run those (read-only audit), so the attribution to these three is likely, not verified.

**Acceptance test:** node tools/shoot.mjs --cold tank /tmp/t.png && node /private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-raynos-projects-game-demos-valkyrie-chronicles-demo-opus/9bfd6309-a467-4f65-a389-55c29ed290ad/scratchpad/fall/inklap.mjs /tmp/t.png — dark-mass interior mean |lap| must fall to 4-7 (currently 15.97) and the share of interior pixels with lap>12 to under 15% (currently 54.5%). tools/artstats.mjs ink should land 2.0-3.2% as a consequence. Guard against the obvious cheat: .../fall/runs.mjs /tmp/t.png must still show >=8% of dark area in runs <=2 px (currently 9.2%) — if that collapses, the linework was deleted rather than the noise.
